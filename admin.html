    How to update this tool
    1. Open your updated PPTX in PowerPoint
    2. Go to File → Export → PNG — save slides to your Mac (PowerPoint exports as PNG, that's fine)
    3. Come back here → Add Slides tab → drag in the exported slide files
    4. Assign slides to products (or add new products with + Add Product)
    5. Click Save & Export JSON → download the updated products.json
    6. Upload the new products.json to your server's data/ folder
    7. The BDM tool (index.html) will immediately show the new products
